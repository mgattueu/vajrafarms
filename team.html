Vajra Family

Our First Team Member

Kankrej Breed of Cow

The Kankrej breed is known for strength, resilience, and a calm natural character that makes it deeply valued in traditional dairy farming. With its distinctive build and hardy constitution, it represents the kind of dependable foundation we believe great farm systems are built on.

At Vajra Farms, this breed reflects more than heritage. It represents endurance, thoughtful care, and the close relationship between animal well-being and honest milk quality. For us, this is where the story of purity begins.

Vajra Family

Another Cherished Member

Sahiwal Breed

Sahiwal cattle are often admired for their graceful frame, resilient temperament, and the strong traditional value they hold in farm families that prioritize careful, natural dairy practices. They bring together calm presence and dependable adaptability.

For Vajra Farms, this breed represents continuity, gentleness, and a deep-rooted connection to mindful animal care. It reflects the belief that quality dairy begins with respect, patience, and an environment where every member of the farm family is nurtured with intention.
